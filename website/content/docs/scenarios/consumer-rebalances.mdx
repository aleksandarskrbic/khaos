---
title: Consumer Rebalances
description: The bundled chaos/rebalance-storm scenario, repeatedly triggering real Kafka consumer group rebalances while traffic is flowing.
---

`chaos/rebalance-storm` runs traffic against a 3-consumer group while repeatedly forcing genuine
group rebalances:

```yaml
topics:
  - name: events
    partitions: 6
    consumers_per_group: 3
    producer_rate: 500
    consumer_delay_ms: 5

incidents:
  - type: rebalance_consumer
    every_seconds: 20
    initial_delay_seconds: 15
```

<Terminal
  lines={[{ type: "command", text: "khaos run chaos/rebalance-storm" }]}
/>

`rebalance_consumer` doesn't just pause a consumer: it closes one matched consumer entirely (a
real departure from the group) and then registers a fresh replacement in its place. That's what
actually triggers the group coordinator to rebalance. Khaos's consumer groups use eager
`Range`/`RoundRobin` assignment rather than cooperative-sticky, so every rebalance here is the
disruptive, stop-the-world kind: every consumer in the group pauses processing while assignment
is recomputed, not just the one that left.

One thing to know before you rely on this for anything beyond "does a rebalance happen": the
replacement consumer reverts to default `failure_rate`/`on_failure`/`max_retries` config, not
whatever the original consumer had configured. If your scenario's failure simulation matters to
what you're testing, keep that in mind across a rebalance.
