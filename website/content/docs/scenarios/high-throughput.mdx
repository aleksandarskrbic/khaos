---
title: High Throughput
description: The bundled traffic/high-throughput scenario, a straightforward Kafka load test tuned for sustained throughput.
---

`traffic/high-throughput` runs two topics, each with 2 producers and 2 consumers, tuned for
throughput rather than latency:

```yaml
topics:
  - name: orders
    partitions: 6
    num_producers: 2
    consumers_per_group: 2
    producer_rate: 1000
    consumer_delay_ms: 0
    producer_config:
      batch_size: 32768
      linger_ms: 10
      acks: "1"
      compression_type: lz4

  - name: events
    partitions: 6
    num_producers: 2
    consumers_per_group: 2
    producer_rate: 1000
    consumer_delay_ms: 0
    producer_config:
      batch_size: 32768
      linger_ms: 10
      acks: "1"
      compression_type: lz4
```

<Terminal
  lines={[{ type: "command", text: "khaos run traffic/high-throughput" }]}
/>

Compared to Khaos's defaults (`batch_size: 16384`, `linger_ms: 5`), this scenario doubles the batch
size and doubles `linger_ms`: larger batches, fewer requests. `consumer_delay_ms: 0` means
consumers process as fast as they can rather than simulating downstream work, so this scenario
isolates producer/broker/consumer throughput from any artificial slowdown.
