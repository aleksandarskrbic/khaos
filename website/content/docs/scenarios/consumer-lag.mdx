`traffic/consumer-lag` runs two topics whose producer rate outpaces what their consumers can
process, given an artificial per-message delay:

```yaml
topics:
  - name: payments
    partitions: 12
    num_producers: 2
    num_consumer_groups: 2
    consumers_per_group: 2
    producer_rate: 2000
    consumer_delay_ms: 50
    message_schema:
      key_distribution: round_robin
      key_cardinality: 100

  - name: notifications
    partitions: 12
    num_producers: 3
    num_consumer_groups: 2
    consumers_per_group: 2
    producer_rate: 3000
    consumer_delay_ms: 100
    message_schema:
      key_distribution: uniform
      key_cardinality: 200
```

<Terminal
  lines={[{ type: "command", text: "khaos run traffic/consumer-lag --lag-poll 5s" }]}
/>

`consumer_delay_ms` is applied per message, per consumer. It's what caps how fast each consumer
can actually process, independent of how fast the producer publishes. With `--lag-poll`, the TUI
shows both self-reported lag (from Khaos's own produced/consumed counters) and real broker-side lag
from committed offsets, side by side.
